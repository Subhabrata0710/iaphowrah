Who We Are

Howrah Academy of Pediatrics

The Howrah Academy of Pediatrics (IAP Howrah) is an active district branch of the Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP). We are a dedicated community of pediatricians working tirelessly to improve child healthcare standards, promote medical education, and foster research in the field of pediatrics within Howrah and across West Bengal.

Our members range from seasoned practitioners to enthusiastic post-graduate trainees, all united by a common goal: ensuring that every child receives the highest standard of medical care.

To achieve optimal health, growth, and development for all infants, children, and adolescents through excellence in pediatric practice, education, and community outreach.

Our Vision
