Our Initiatives

What Does IAP Howrah Do?

We actively engage in activities that promote child health, educate medical professionals, and serve the community.

📚

Academic Programs

Organizing regular Continuing Medical Education (CME) sessions, clinical meetings, and workshops to keep practitioners updated with the latest in pediatrics.

🤝

Community Service

Conducting health camps, awareness programs, and vaccination drives in rural and urban areas to ensure healthcare reaches the underserved.

🎓

PGT Training

Providing specialized training modules, mentorship, and platform for paper presentations for Post Graduate Trainees.
