About The Event

The Premier State Pediatric Conference

The 45th West Bengal Pediatric Conference (WB PEDICON 2026) is the flagship annual event organized by the West Bengal Academy of Pediatrics, hosted this year by IAP Howrah.

This two-day conference brings together leading pediatricians, researchers, and post-graduate trainees to discuss the latest advancements, guidelines, and challenges in pediatric healthcare.

  • Comprehensive scientific sessions and orations
  • Interactive case discussions and panels
  • Paper and poster presentations for PGTs
  • Networking with peers and senior faculty
